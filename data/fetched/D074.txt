Cite as: San Diego Zoning Code § 98.1103 · Text as of 2026-09-23
(a) It is unlawful for a person to sell, license, or otherwise provide an algorithmic device to a landlord .
(b) It is unlawful for a landlord to use an algorithmic device to set rental rates or occupancy levels for residential rental property .
For each month a violation of section 98.1103(b) exists or continues, and for each residential rental property a landlord uses an algorithmic device , it shall constitute a separate and distinct violation.
(“Use and Sale of Algorithmic Devices Prohibited” added 5-22-2025 by O-21955 N.S.; effective 6-21-2025.)
