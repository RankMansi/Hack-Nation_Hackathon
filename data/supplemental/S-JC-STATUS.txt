AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 218 (MULTIPLE DWELLINGS) OF THE CODE OF 
ORDINANCES OF THE CITY OF JERSEY CITY BY ADDING SUBSECTION 3 “MANDATORY 
DISCLOSURE FOR RENT INCREASES” TO SECTION 12 AND CORRECTING LANGUAGE IN 
SECTION 1.

COUNCIL AS A WHOLE offered and moved adoption of the following ordinance:

Whereas, Jersey City is facing an affordability crisis with rents increasing dramatically annually and thousands of 
residents receiving eviction notices; and

Whereas, the Jersey City Municipal Council adopted ordinance 25-057 banning the use of algorithmic rent-setting 
and the use of any algorithmic rent-setting device; and

Whereas, the municipal council believes that all tenants and landlords in the city should be made aware of this ban 
and the rights of tenants.

Whereas, having landlords proactively declare that they are not violating the law will ensure tenants know their 
rights and landlords are fully aware of their responsibilities and can not claim otherwise; and

Whereas, a typographical error in Chapter 218, Section 1(A) requires correction.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT FURTHER ORDAINED BY THE CITY COUNCIL OF THE CITY OF JERSEY 
CITY THAT: that Chapter 218 (MULTIPLE DWELLINGS) Section 1 (UNIFORMED SECURITY SERVICE) of 
the Municipal Code shall be amended to read as follows:

APPROVED AS TO LEGAL FORM

A. Every owner or agent of an owner of a multiple dwelling of 100 or more dwelling units shall furnish a minimum 
of one uniformed security guard for each residential building in the property or community upon its premises 
twenty-four (24) hours a day, seven days a week. The owner or agent of the multiple dwelling shall post conspicuous 
signage inside the common areas and entrance points of the building that shall provide the direct onsite contact 
information of the uniformed security guards present. For the purposes of this chapter, a "uniformed security guard" 
shall consist of a Licensed New Jersey Security Guard, as specified in N.J.S.A. § 45:19A-1 et seq. and N.J.A.C. 
§13:55A-1.1 et seq, hired for the purpose of protection of persons or property, real or personal, from injury or harm 
that wears a particular set of clothes that clearly identifies them as a security guard.

Ord. 25-098 An Ordinance amending Chapter 218 (Multiple Dwellings) of the Code of Ordinances of the City of Jersey City by adding Subsection 3 “Mandatory Disclosure for Rent Increases” to Section 12 and correcting language in Section 1.

3. Mandatory Disclosure for Rent Increases:
a. Any landlord must include, with any lease or written notice of rent increase for a residential dwelling unit, a sworn disclosure statement, executed under penalty of perjury, in substantially the following form: MANDATORY ALGORITHMIC RENT-SETTING DISCLOSURE Pursuant to Jersey City Code § 218-12 I, the undersigned, being the landlord or an authorized agent of the landlord for the property located at ____________________, for dwelling unit ______, hereby certify under the penalty of perjury that:
1. No entity subscribed to, contracted with, or otherwise exchanged anything of value in return for the services of a Service Provider, in accordance with Chapter 218-12.2, in connection with the rent amount of $______ to $_______ proposed on ____________..
2. The methodology used to determine this rent increase did not make use of any shared non-public competitor data.
3. I understand that, as elaborated in the Truth in Renting Guide and N.J.S.A. 56:8-2, any deception, fraud, misrepresentation, to or knowing failure or the refusal provide important information in connection with advertisement of real estate, including this lease, is illegal in New Jersey under the Consumer Fraud Act, N.J.S.A. 56:8-1 et seq. 3 4.Enforcement NO CHANGES
4. 5. Severability. NO CHANGES
